Nitish Singh portfolio (multi page)
Pages: index.html, projects.html, experience.html, manage.html (private, noindex)

Publish: drag this whole folder onto Netlify.

Before publishing, replace https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app in sitemap.xml and robots.txt
with your real website address, so search engines can read your sitemap.

Change content
- assets/js/data.js holds skills, experience, education and projects.
- manage.html (sign in) lets you add items in your own browser.
  To show them to every visitor: Export, paste the JSON into the "custom" block
  at the bottom of data.js, and upload the site again.
- Chatbot answers live in the KB list in assets/js/app.js.

Contact form: sends through Formspree (mqagozea). Each email includes the sender's
details, approximate location, time zone, device and page, as separate fields.
